## B2. Codebook (Open Coding Categories)

**Instructions:** Apply codes to meaningful units of text (phrases, sentences, or short paragraphs). Multiple codes may be applied to one excerpt. Codes are used to identify patterns that later form themes.

---

### 1) Encounter Experience

* **ENCOUNTER_CONTEXT** — Describes where/when the encounter happened (general setting).
* **ENCOUNTER_SEQUENCE** — Describes the sequence of events and key moments.
* **SPECIES_ID_CONFIDENCE** — Indicates confidence or uncertainty about which animal was involved.

### 2) Risk and Decision-Making

* **RISK_PERCEPTION_LOW / MODERATE / HIGH** — Expresses perceived risk level and rationale.
* **UNCERTAINTY** — Describes uncertainty about what to do or what might happen.
* **DECISION_DRIVER** — Explains influences on decisions (fear, prior knowledge, advice, signage, etc.).
* **SAFETY_STRATEGY** — Actions taken to stay safe (avoidance, distance, leaving area).
* **ESCALATION_CONCERN** — Concern about provoking animal or worsening situation.

### 3) Information Needs (During Encounter)

* **NEED_STEP_BY_STEP** — Desire for clear “do/don’t” or stepwise guidance.
* **NEED_SPECIES_BEHAVIOR** — Desire for information about animal behavior.
* **NEED_DANGER_SIGNS** — Desire for warning signs and risk cues.
* **NEED_CONTACTS** — Desire for who to call/report to and how.
* **NEED_AFTERCARE** — Desire for post-encounter steps (reporting, medical, vehicle/property, etc.).

### 4) Prevention and Preparedness

* **PREVENTION_ALERTS** — Desire for alerts or push notifications.
* **PREVENTION_ROUTE** — Desire for route guidance or hotspot avoidance.
* **PREVENTION_SEASONAL** — Desire for seasonal/time-based risk information.
* **PREVENTION_EDUCATION** — Desire for education, tips, or preparedness resources.
* **SELF_EFFICACY** — Confidence or lack of confidence in handling encounters.

### 5) App Feature Preferences

* **FEATURE_HELP_NOW** — Preference for immediate guidance interface.
* **FEATURE_REPORTING** — Preference for reporting features (photo, details, categories).
* **FEATURE_MAP** — Preference for map-based sightings display.
* **FEATURE_OFFLINE_LOW_DATA** — Need for offline or low-data usability.
* **FEATURE_PERSONALIZATION** — Desire for context-aware or tailored guidance.
* **FEATURE_SIMPLE_UI** — Desire for fast, simple interface suitable under stress.

### 6) Trust, Governance, and Ethics

* **TRUST_MANAGER** — Who should manage the app/data and why.
* **TRUST_ACCURACY** — Concern about information quality and reliability.
* **PRIVACY_LOCATION** — Location privacy concerns and preferences.
* **PRIVACY_IDENTITY** — Identity privacy concerns and preferences.
* **DATA_MISUSE_WILDLIFE** — Concern that wildlife location data could be misused.
* **SURVEILLANCE_CONCERN** — Concern about surveillance or tracking.

### 7) Adoption Barriers

* **BARRIER_CONNECTIVITY** — Connectivity, data cost, signal limitations.
* **BARRIER_COMPLEXITY** — Too slow/complex to use during an encounter.
* **BARRIER_CONSEQUENCES** — Fear of negative consequences of reporting/using app.
* **BARRIER_RELEVANCE** — Belief that encounters are too rare or app not needed.
* **BARRIER_CULTURAL_BELIEF** — Beliefs/values that shape attitudes toward wildlife and technology.

### 8) Desired Outcomes

* **DESIRED_OUTCOME_SAFETY** — Goal of physical safety and harm reduction.
* **DESIRED_OUTCOME_CONFIDENCE** — Reduced anxiety, increased certainty.
* **DESIRED_OUTCOME_COEXISTENCE** — Avoiding harm to wildlife; humane response.
* **DESIRED_OUTCOME_COMMUNITY** — Shared awareness, community benefit, collective safety.
